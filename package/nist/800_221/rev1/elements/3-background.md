This section discusses ICT risk considerations and is structured according to the six steps in the notional ICTRM life cycle described in Fig. 2:

- 1\. Identify the context.

- 2\. Identify the risks.

- 3\. Analyze (qualify) the risks.

- 4\. Prioritize the risks.

- 5\. Plan and execute risk response strategies.

- 6\. Monitor, evaluate, and adjust risk management.

Following those, Sec. 3.8 briefly discusses considerations for positive risks.
