Risk analysis for ICT tends to be inconsistent compared to many other forms of risk. Even where guidelines are provided, such as in NIST publications, the resulting risk assessment reports from agencies differ significantly. Moreover, foundational inputs for likelihood and impact calculations generally lack a standardized methodology or are at the discretion of vendors who provide a scoring system. Decisions are often made based on an individual’s instinct, experience, and knowledge of conventional wisdom and typical practices. In addition, there is usually little analysis carried out after controls are deployed to determine whether risks have been reduced to a level deemed acceptable (i.e., within the established risk tolerance parameters).
