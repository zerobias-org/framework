While risk models have historically focused on protecting against adversarial events, organizations may not have sufficiently explored positive risk: the deliberate identification, tracking, and pursuit of uncertain opportunities that could beneficially impact the pursuit of enterprise objectives. Like negative risks, which often trigger executive attention and response protocols, positive risks (e.g., emerging market shifts, disruptive technology adoption curves, untapped partnership possibilities) should also find a home in enterprise risk registers. Failure to do so can have measurable consequences. For example, companies that did not systematically monitor and act on opportunities around cloud computing, generative SI, or platform-based business models may have lost some competitive advantage. In addition to the need to invest in people, processes, and technologies to address potential threats and vulnerabilities, it is important to identify and intentionally pursue potential staffing, innovations, and other strategic advantages that could provide benefits.
