Enterprises should integrate C-SCRM into the contingency planning policy and related SCRM Strategy/Implementation Plan, policies, and SCRM Plan. The policy should cover information systems and the supply chain network and, at a minimum, address scenarios such as:

- a. Unplanned component failure and subsequent replacement;
- b. Planned replacement related to feature improvements, maintenance, upgrades, and modernization; and
- c. Product and/or service disruption.
