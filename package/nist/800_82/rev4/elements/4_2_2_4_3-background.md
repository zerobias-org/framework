OT environments often depend on third-party communication providers for connectivity to geographically remote sites and with external partners. These providers may lack adequate network protection, leaving them vulnerable to threats that degrade their availability and integrity. Therefore, organizations should deploy technical redundancies and contingency plans to support operations if these providers are compromised and unavailable, including establishing backup and contingency communications capabilities (e.g., PACE plans) or establishing procedures to operate in an isolated or manual mode if these communications are unavailable.
