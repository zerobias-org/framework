If cloud-based or hosted services (e.g., remote access platforms, cloud-based historians, vendor remote monitoring services) are present in the OT architecture, the log sources they generate should be included in the organization’s host-based logging inventory. Unlike device-level logs, which originate from hardware within the OT environment, service logs are generated and may be retained by an external platform. This introduces considerations around log accessibility, retention controls, and data sovereignty that organizations should account for when defining their overall logging requirements.
