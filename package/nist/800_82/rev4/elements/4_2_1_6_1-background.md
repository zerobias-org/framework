While password authentication schemes are extremely common, they present challenges in OT environments. Device protocols may transmit passwords in plaintext, and complex passwords could pose safety issues if operators are locked out during critical events.

Organizations should consider the following password recommendations:

- Change default passwords. Always change default passwords on OT equipment to prevent trivial exploitation.
- Balance complexity and safety. Enforce appropriate password length and complexity, balancing security requirements with the operational necessity for rapid access during safety-critical functions.
- Protect privileged accounts. Administrative accounts require stronger complexity, more frequent rotation, and additional physical safeguards.
- Secure transmission and storage. Passwords should never be transmitted over the network unless protected by FIPS-approved encryption. Organizations should also consider using a secure, centralized password management tool.
- Life cycle management. Implement procedures to rotate passwords periodically, when a compromise is suspected, or when an individual leaves the organization
