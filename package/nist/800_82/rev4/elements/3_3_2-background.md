Organizations should define the required processes, technical mitigations, and evidence expected from a supplier of any OT component. This can include:

- 1\. Criteria for secure software development and life cycle management
- 2\. Service level agreements (SLAs) that document the supplier’s approach to the notification and resolution of vulnerabilities
- 3\. Defined maintenance and support agreements to determine acceptable methods for vendor remote access (if necessary), SLAs for issue resolution and incident response (if applicable), and obligate the third party to comply with the organization’s access control procedures when on-site
- 4\. Technical mechanisms regarding the hardware and software components within the products, such as through HBOMs and SBOMs
- 5\. Digital artifacts to validate the authenticity of vendor-provided OT hardware, software, and firmware, such as digital signatures
- 6\. Clear incentive structures in supplier contracts to encourage and reward strong security practices
