Asset management depends on understanding individual assets, their architecture, data flows, and associated operational functions. Accurate inventory information supports multiple risk management objectives, including risk assessment, vulnerability management, and

obsolescence tracking. Asset inventories are also critical to supporting other security functions, including detection and response/recovery.

This section will explore key challenges and recommendations for establishing an asset inventory. While asset management also encompasses the security configuration and maintenance associated with assets, the discussion of these topics is included in Sec. 4.2.3.
