Managing OT hardware throughout the system life cycle is essential to ensuring operational efficiency and reducing the organization’s attack surface. When OT devices are

decommissioned, associated risks may manifest as many devices participate in tightly coupled control and safety functions. These devices may also have dependencies that are unknown to the maintenance staff (e.g., time synchronization, OPC links, software licensing). When the decision is made to decommission a system, a thorough review must be conducted to ensure that the asset’s removal from production has no negative or otherwise adverse effects on normal operations (e.g., no impact on system functionality, overall monitoring, or operator visibility). If the review yields no findings of note, staff may proceed and remove the system from production. It is recommended that operators revoke devices’ certificates or other keys, disable device-related and service accounts, remove firewall and ACL entries, and ensure that the device is no longer reachable via the network or other logical means upon removal.
