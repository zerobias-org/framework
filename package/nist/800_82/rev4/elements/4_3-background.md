Situational awareness of the OT system is imperative for understanding its current state, validating that it is operating as intended, and ensuring that no policy violations or cyber incidents have hindered its operation. To detect security incidents across the OT environment, the organization should deploy infrastructure to monitor and collect data from networks, hosts, users, and physical access systems. In addition to collecting this data, the organization should ensure that it is properly collected, stored, and reviewed by security analysts to help identify and investigate potential intrusions.
