There are several possible factors for determining the authenticity of a person, device, or system, including something you know (e.g., PIN, password), something you have (e.g., key, dongle, smart card), something you are (i.e., a biological characteristic, such as a fingerprint or retinal signature), and where you are (e.g., geofencing).

OT organizations must manage user accounts and authentication mechanisms across diverse environments, and many device manufacturers and legacy devices lack support for modern authentication mechanisms. Therefore, organizations are often forced to share user accounts, which limits both the accountability of user access and the ability to have individual user credentials. Organizations must also often provide accounts and credentials for manufacturers, integrators, or contractors that require remote or on-site access.
