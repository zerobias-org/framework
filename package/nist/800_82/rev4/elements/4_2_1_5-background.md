As organizations modernize their industrial environments, they may find opportunities to streamline account administration by centralizing user, device, and process identification and authentication. Utilizing common network technologies, such as Active Directory or Lightweight Directory Access Protocol (LDAP), can support centralizing identity management across both IT and OT environments. This centralization enables the convergence of physical and logical access controls. For example, standardizing on federal PIV smart cards allows an organization to use the same credential mechanism for both physical entry into a facility and logical authentication into OT assets, adjusting the required authentication factors (e.g., Card-Only vs. Card+PIN) based on the risk level of the protected resource. However, while unified credentialing improves monitoring capabilities and reduces administrative overhead, organizations must carefully weigh the operational benefits of centralized accounts against the increased cybersecurity risks of allowing authenticated accounts from the IT environment to access the OT environment.
