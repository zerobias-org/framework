The ability to create, modify, or delete user accounts needs to be managed within an organization. Separating the approver of these changes from the implementor provides an additional layer of protection, ensuring that no single person can compromise the system. Multiple approvers may be required for accounts with higher levels of privilege. For example, creating a normal user account may only require their manager’s approval, but creating a system administrator may require their direct manager and a security officer to confirm that additional background checks have been performed.
