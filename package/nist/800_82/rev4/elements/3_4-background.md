Organizational leadership should develop and disseminate policies that establish security requirements for their environments. These policies may include the identification and assignment of roles, responsibilities, management commitment, and compliance. The policies may also reflect coordination among the organizational entities responsible for the different aspects of security (e.g., technical, physical, personnel, cyber-physical, access control, media protection, vulnerability management, maintenance, monitoring).

Where possible, OT-specific security policies and procedures should be compatible and coordinated with other groups and existing policies and procedures throughout the organization. This includes coordination with IT management, human resources, and organizational governance teams. Examples of where policy coordination is necessary across groups include:

- IT: When IT infrastructure is used to provide user access to OT infrastructure or protect OT data along with processes to investigate and respond to security incidents that potentially span both OT and IT networks
- Human resources: The investigation or remediation of security issues regarding organizational personnel
- Organization governance: The incorporation of necessary regulatory requirements, decision authorities, and reporting to facilitate awareness

The development of cybersecurity policies should be based on a risk assessment that will set the security priorities and goals for the organization. Procedures that support the policies should be developed so that the policies are implemented fully and properly for OT. Cybersecurity procedures should also be documented, tested, and updated periodically in response to changes in policy, technology, and threats.

Supplemental guidance for cybersecurity policy can be found in the following documents:

- SP 800-39, Managing Information Security Risk: Organization, Mission, and Information System View
- SP 800-37r2, Risk Management Framework for Information Systems and Organizations: A System Life Cycle Approach for Security and Privacy
- SP 800-100, Information Security Handbook: A Guide for Managers
- IR 8286r1, Integrating Cybersecurity and Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- IR 8183Av2, Cybersecurity Framework Manufacturing Profile Low Impact Level Example Implementations Guide
