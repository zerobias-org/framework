Within OT environments, the primary objective of the CSF 2.0 PROTECT (PR) function is to develop, implement, and sustain appropriate safeguards to manage the organization’s cybersecurity risk without compromising the safety, reliability, or availability of continuous physical processes. Achieving this requires organizations to carefully tailor and apply four core control families: identity, authentication, and access control (PR.AA); data security (PR.DS); platform security (PR.PS); and technology infrastructure resilience (PR.IR). This section provides specific guidelines on implementing these control objectives and addressing the unique operational constraints, legacy system limitations, and safety imperatives inherent to OT environments. 6
