The IAL2 Non-Biometric Pathway provides verification methods that do not use an automated comparison of biometric samples provided by the applicant. This pathway can still involve the collection and verification of biometric data (e.g., visual comparison to a facial image contained on identity evidence performed by a proofing agent), but such comparisons are done through manual rather than automated means. Additional verification methods that do not require the use of automated biometric comparison are also included in the Digital Evidence Pathway requirements specified in Sec. 4.2.6.2.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP communicates its use of the Non-Biometric Pathway to all RPs that use its service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that it communicates its use of the Non-Biometric Pathway for verification to the RPs that use its service.
