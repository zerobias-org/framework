**Assessment objective**

Confirm the CSP trains its agents to identify signs that an applicant has been coerced or manipulated into participating in the identity proofing process.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy for training its agents to identify signs of coercion, manipulation, or social engineering.
- INTERVIEW: Interview one or more agents of the CSP to determine the training they've received to identify these signs.
