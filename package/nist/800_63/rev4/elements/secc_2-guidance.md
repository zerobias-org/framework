Assessment is required when: The RP does not request or process personal information.

RPs that request or process personal information must implement security controls at the moderate baseline or higher (see SECC-3).

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP employs appropriately tailored security controls from at least the low baseline defined in SP 800-53 or an equivalent standard.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine evidence that the RP has been assessed against at least the low baseline security controls defined in SP 800-53 or an equivalent standard. Verify that the resulting authorization or certification is current and covers all systems supporting federation services.
