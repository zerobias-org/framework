**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 2.4.3 B

If CSPs or IdPs process attributes for purposes other than identity services (i.e., identity proofing, authentication, or attribute assertions), related fraud mitigation, or compliance with laws or legal processes, they SHALL implement measures to maintain predictability and manageability commensurate with the privacy risks that arise from the additional processing.
