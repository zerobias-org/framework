**Applies to:** RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.11.1 D

The RP SHALL protect itself against the injection of manufactured or captured assertion references by using cross-site scripting (XSS) and cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection, rejecting assertion references outside of the correct stage of a federation transaction, or other accepted techniques discussed in Sec. 3.11.1.
