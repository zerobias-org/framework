**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP employs a mechanism to analyze whether submitted digital media contains artifacts or other indicators of having been modified or forged.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's design or other documentation to determine that it employs mechanisms for detecting indicators of digital media tampering or forgery.
- TEST: Test the CSP's identity proofing workflow by attempting to submit an image or other type of media that has been digitally modified or forged.
