**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP provides a list of acceptable evidence of the relationship between an applicant and their applicant reference prior to initiating the relationship confirmation process.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that the CSP provides a list of acceptable evidence of the relationship between an applicant and their applicant reference prior to initiating the relationship confirmation process.
