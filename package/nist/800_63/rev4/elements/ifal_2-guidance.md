Dependent on the results from controls AL-1 and IFAL-1a.

Assessment is required when: the online service is assessed to be "high-impact."

**Assessment objective**

If the online service is "high impact," determine if further assessment has been done to evaluate the risk of a compromised IdP and if FAL2 or FAL3 is more appropriate.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the documentation for additional assessments that evaluate if FAL2 or FAL3 is more appropriate.
