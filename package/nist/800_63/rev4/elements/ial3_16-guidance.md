**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP's proofing agents participate remotely in the evidence collection, evidence validation, and identity verification steps of kiosk-based on-site attended identity proofing at IAL3.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its agents participate remotely in the evidence collection, evidence validation, and identity verification steps for kiosk-based on-site attended identity proofing at IAL3.
- INTERVIEW: Interview one or more proofing agents of the CSP to determine that, for kiosk-based on-site attended identity proofing, they are required to participate remotely, at a minimum, in the evidence collection, evidence validation, and identity verification steps of the identity proofing process for IAL3.
