This is the IdP counterpart to RR-1 (RP Subscriber Redress). This control addresses redress for matters solely within the IdP's control.

"Clear and accessible" means the redress mechanism must be easy to find and use. Complex processes, hidden contact information, or technical jargon that prevents subscribers from understanding how to seek redress would fail this requirement.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP provides clear and accessible means for subscribers to seek redress for matters under the IdP's control.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine IdP documentation, subscriber-facing interfaces, and help resources for redress mechanisms. Verify that redress mechanisms are clearly presented and accessible to subscribers, and that they address subscriber account usage in federation transactions, attribute values, and, if applicable, derived attribute values, IdP functionality, holder-of-key authenticators, and IdP allowlists. Review IdP documentation to determine whether there are any other items under the IdP's control, and if so, verify that redress mechanisms are sufficient.
- TEST: Test by locating and initiating the redress process to verify clarity and accessibility.
