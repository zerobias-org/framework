**Assessment objective**

Determine that the CSP has a process for communicating summarized results of its privacy risk assessment to its RPs and that such summaries are in sufficient detail to allow those RPs to determine if the risks of using the CSP's identity services are acceptable.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's privacy risk or other documentation, such as agreements or contracts, to determine how it communicates the results of its risk assessment to the RPs that use its services.
