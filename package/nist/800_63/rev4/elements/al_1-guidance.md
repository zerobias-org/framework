**Assessment objective**

Determine if the online service needs an IAL, AAL, or FAL.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the documented needs of the identity service, the results of the DIRM documentation completed to date, and the IAL requirements from 800-63A, to determine if identity proofing is necessary.
- EXAMINE: Examine the documented needs of the identity service, the results of the DIRM documentation completed to date, and the IAL requirements from 800-63B, to determine if authentication is needed.
- EXAMINE: Examine the documented needs of the identity service, the results of the DIRM documentation completed to date, and the IAL requirements from 800-63C, to determine if federation is needed.
