**Applies to:** Verifier · **Assurance level:** AAL3 · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 2.3.1 A

AAL3 authentication SHALL require an authenticator combination consisting of either multi-factor cryptographic authentication (Sec. 3.1.7); or  single-factor cryptographic authentication (Sec. 3.1.6) used in conjunction with either a password (Sec. 3.1.1) or a biometric comparison.
