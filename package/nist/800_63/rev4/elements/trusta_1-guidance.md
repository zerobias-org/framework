For pre-established trust agreement requirements, see PETA series (Sec. 4.3.1).

For subscriber-driven trust agreement requirements, see SDTAE series (Sec. 4.3.2).

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP uses one of the two trust agreement establishment methods.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the relevant trust agreement artifact(s) to verify that the establishment method is either pre-established by the federated parties prior to the federation transaction, or subscriber-driven during the federation transaction.
