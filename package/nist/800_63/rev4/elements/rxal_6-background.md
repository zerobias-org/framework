**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 2.5 F

If the xAL is unchanging for all messages between the IdP and RP (e.g., enterprise scenarios in which all subscribers are identity-proofed at the same IAL and use the same authenticator type), the xAL information SHALL be included in the terms of the trust agreement between the IdP and RP.
