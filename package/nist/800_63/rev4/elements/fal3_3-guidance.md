FAL3 prohibits dynamic or automated identifier establishment. Party identifiers must be manually configured through direct administrative action. This requirement reduces automated attack vectors and ensures human verification of party identity before establishing FAL3 connections.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether identifiers for all federation parties are established through manual processes at FAL3.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine configuration documentation to confirm that federation identifiers (e.g., entity IDs, issuer URIs) for the CSP, IdP, and RP are manually configured by authorized personnel.  Verify that dynamic registration (Sec. 4.4.2) is not enabled for FAL3 transactions.
