Assessment of attribute removal upon RP subscriber account termination is performed under RPSA-1, which covers the full scope of data removal, including federated identifiers, bound authenticators, attributes, and identity information. Exceptions must be documented with a specific regulatory, legal, policy, or risk-based justification; general data retention policies are insufficient.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP removes all subscriber attributes from storage when the RP subscriber account is terminated, except when restricted by regulations, laws, policies, or application risk requirements.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Satisfied by RPSA-1.
