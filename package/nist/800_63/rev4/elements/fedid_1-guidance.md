Assessment is required when: Federated identifiers are used.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP/CSP assigns identifiers that are unique to each subscriber.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: This is a definitional statement, not an independently assessable requirement. Subject identifier uniqueness is an inherent property of any functioning IdP or CSP identity system. Enforcement of federated identifier uniqueness is satisfied by FEDID-2.
