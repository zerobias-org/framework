Assessment is required when: the CSP/IdP used by the RP has created a DIAS.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if the RP reviewed the CSP/IdP's DIAS and incorporated relevant information into the RP's DIAS for each online service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine each RP online service's DIAS for relevant information from the DIAS of the CSP/IdP the RP intends to use.
