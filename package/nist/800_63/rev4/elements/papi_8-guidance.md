Assessment is required when: The IdP provides a provisioning API.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP is responsible for the content and accuracy of the provisioning API, including when the API infrastructure is operated by a third party.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the system architecture documentation to determine whether the provisioning API is operated directly by the IdP or by a third party. If it is operated by a third party, examine the agreement between the IdP and the third party to verify that the IdP retains responsibility for the content and accuracy of the API's outputs. Verify that the agreement defines how the IdP enforces content and accuracy requirements (e.g., attribute validation, error correction, audit rights).
