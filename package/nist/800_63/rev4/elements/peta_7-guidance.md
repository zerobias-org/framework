**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the trust agreement is periodically reviewed, and that, as part of that review, subscriber data collection and data exchanges are assessed to determine whether they are still necessary.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine relevant documentation to verify that the cadence of trust agreement reviews is documented and that clear ownership for the review is assigned. Determine whether the periodic reviews address whether the trust agreement remains fit for purpose and whether subscriber data exchange and collection remain necessary. For trust agreements that have been in effect long enough for a review cycle to have occurred, verify that reviews have been conducted as scheduled.
