Assessment is required when: Authentication is required at both the IdP and the RP.

Note:

This control was intended to apply to both HoK and bound authenticator assertions.

When the errata corrections are issued, this control will be updated to read:

"The RP SHALL process and validate the assertion in addition to verifying the authenticator."

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP validates the assertion from the IdP when processing transactions that require RP authenticator validation.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Satisfied by CFAL-2.
