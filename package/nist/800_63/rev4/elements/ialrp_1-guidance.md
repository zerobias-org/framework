Assessment is required when: The IdP utilizes an allowlist for RPs.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether RPs in an IdP's allowlist are compliant with the terms of their trust agreement.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the IdP's policies and procedures for adding RPs to the allowlist to verify that a process exists to confirm RP compliance with the trust agreement artifact(s) before allowlisting. For a sample of allowlisted RPs, verify that evidence of compliance exists (e.g., review records, attestations, federation authority approval).
