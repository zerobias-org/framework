**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** IAL2 · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 4.2.12 #1

The CSP SHALL provide the ability for the applicant to bind an authenticator using one of the following methods:

- (a) Remote enrollment of a subscriber-provided authenticator consistent with the requirements for the authenticator type, as defined in Sec. 4.1.3 of [SP800-63B].
- (b) Distribution of a physical authenticator to a validated address.
- (c) Distribution or on-site enrollment of an authenticator.
