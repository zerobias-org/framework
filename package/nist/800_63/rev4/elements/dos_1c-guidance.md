The purpose of this initial step is to establish a common understanding of the online service's context and functionality, which is essential for informing the subsequent risk assessment steps of the DIRM process.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if a description of the online service has been developed that includes the legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements, including obligations that apply to the online service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the organization's DIRM documentation for each online service to ensure it includes its  legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements, including obligations that apply to the online service.
