For more information about SORNs, see OPM's System of Records Notice (SORN) Guide(https://www.opm.gov/information-management/privacy-policy/privacy-references/sornguide.pdf).

**Assessment objective**

Determine that the Federal Agency has  published a SORN, as applicable, for its use of an identity service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the Federal Agency's documentation to determine that it has published a SORN for its identity service(s).
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine that it has published a SORN for its identity service(s).
