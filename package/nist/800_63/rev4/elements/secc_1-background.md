**Applies to:** CSP/IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 3.11 A

IdPs and CSPs SHALL employ appropriately tailored security controls from at least the moderate baseline security controls defined in [SP800-53] or an equivalent federal (e.g., [FEDRAMP]) or industry standard that the organization has determined for the information systems, applications, and online services that these guidelines are used to protect.
