The purpose of this initial step is to establish a common understanding of the online service's context and functionality, which is essential for informing the subsequent risk assessment steps of the DIRM process.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if a description of the online service has been developed that includes the organizational mission and business objectives that are supported by the online service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the organization's DIRM documentation for each online service to ensure it includes a description of its organizational mission and  business objectives.
