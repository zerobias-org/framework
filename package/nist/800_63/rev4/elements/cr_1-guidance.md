Assessment is required when: The trust relationship between the RP and CSP is facilitated by a federation authority.

In subscriber-controlled wallet ecosystems, the CSP and RP typically do not communicate directly. A federation authority can bridge this gap by defining ecosystem-wide trust agreement artifact(s) that govern the relationships among RPs, CSPs, and wallets.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the federation authority's trust agreement artifacts for a subscriber-controlled wallet ecosystem contains the required terms.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the federation authority trust agreement artifact(s) to verify all required terms are present.
