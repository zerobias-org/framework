CSPs MAY use one-to-many (1:N) identification in support of resolution or deduplication, pursuant to a privacy risk assessment.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if the CSP employs 1:N identification and, if it does, confirm the algorithms meet or surpass the stated performance threshold.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's biometric testing results to determine that use of any 1:N scenarios meet or surpass the stated performance threshold.
