**Assessment objective**

For remote attended video sessions, confirm the CSP requires that the applicant is visible to the proofing agent for each step of the identity proofing process.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its requirements for applicant visibility during remote attended identity proofing sessions.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine the process for viewing applicants during each step of remote attended identity proofing sessions.
