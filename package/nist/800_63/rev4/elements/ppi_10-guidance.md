Assessment is required when: Pairwise Pseudonymous Identifiers (PPIs) are used and common pairwise identifiers are requested by RPs.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether only RPs that have been explicitly configured to use a common identifier are given the common identifier.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine by comparing the IdP's configured shared PPI sets against the authorized RP sets documented in the trust agreement artifact(s). Confirm that no RP appears in a shared PPI set without corresponding authorization.
