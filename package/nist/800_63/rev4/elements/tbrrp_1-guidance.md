Assessment is required when: The RP uses time-based removal of RP subscriber accounts.

"Sufficient notice" should be tailored to expected usage patterns at the RP. For example, an RP that expects weekly access might provide 30 days' notice, while an RP that expects monthly access might provide 90 days or more.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP provides sufficient notice to subscribers about pending account termination and an option to reactivate the account prior to scheduled termination, when processing inactive accounts for time-based removal.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the RP's policies and procedures for time-based account termination, including notice timelines and reactivation processes. Also, examine sample termination notices to verify they inform subscribers of pending termination and provide a reactivation option.
- TEST: Test by triggering a time-based termination process for a test account and verifying that notice is provided, and reactivation is available prior to termination.
