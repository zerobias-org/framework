**Applies to:** CSP/IdP/RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 3.5 K

The terms of the trust agreement SHALL be reviewed by all parties (e.g., the CSP, IdP, RP, or a federation authority) that are responsible for informing the subscriber of the terms of the trust agreement before the disclosure to the subscriber occurs in order to avoid revealing sensitive security information.
