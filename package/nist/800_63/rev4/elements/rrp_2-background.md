**Applies to:** Verifier · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 2.4.2 B

If the verifier opts to retain records in the absence of mandatory requirements, the verifier or the CSP or IdP of which it is a part SHALL conduct a risk management process [NISTRMF], including assessments of privacy and security risks, to determine how long records should be retained and SHALL inform the subscriber of that retention policy.
