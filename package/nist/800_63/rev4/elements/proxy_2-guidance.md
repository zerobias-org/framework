Assessment is required when: Proxied federation is used.

A proxy needs to fulfill all of the normative requirements for both RPs and IdPs in order to be considered compliant, in addition to any proxy-specific requirements.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether a proxy functions as both a compliant IdP and a compliant RP.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: This is a summative control. The proxy is assessed against all applicable IdP controls for its IdP-facing role and all applicable RP controls for its RP-facing role.
