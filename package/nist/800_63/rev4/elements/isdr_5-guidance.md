The policy for identity evidence collection and classification of evidence acceptance allows open communication between the CSP and the RP, which can then be communicated to the applicant to build trust between the applicant and RP as the identity evidence collection is a sensitive process.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP has documented the types of evidence it accepts and the justification for how the evidence fulfills the strength requirements of the specified IAL.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine the types of evidence it accepts and the justification for how the types fulfill the strength requirements for the specified IAL.
