**Applies to:** Organization · **Topic:** Privacy, Customer Experience, Threat Resistance · **Section:** SP 800-63-4 3.4.1 A

When progressing from the initial assurance level selection in [the Baseline Controls requirements] (Sec. 3.3.4) to the final xAL selection and implementation, organizations SHALL conduct detailed assessments of the controls defined for the initially selected xALs to identify potential impacts in the operational environment.
