Assessment is required when: The subscriber-controlled wallet is hosted as a remote service.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the AAL of the subscriber's current session at the hosted wallet service is conveyed through the assertion contents or the applicable trust agreement artifact(s).

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine sample assertions and/or trust agreement artifact(s) to confirm the AAL of the subscriber's session at the hosted wallet service is conveyed.
