Assessment is required when: The RP collects additional attributes not provided by the IdP.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP provides a secure and effective means of redress for subscribers to request updates to or removal of additionally collected attributes.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the RP's subscriber-facing interfaces and account management capabilities to verify that subscribers can request updates to, and removal of, any additionally collected attributes from their RP subscriber account.
- TEST: Test by requesting an update to an attribute value and requesting the removal of an attribute, as a test subscriber who has additionally collected attributes in their RP subscriber account. Verify that both requests are processed.
