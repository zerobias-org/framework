**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.6.1.1 D

IdP allowlists SHALL uniquely identify RPs through fully qualified domain names, cryptographic keys, or other identifiers that are applicable to the federation protocol in use. Any entities that share an identifier SHALL be considered equivalent for the purposes of the allowlist. An allowlist entry for an RP SHALL NOT use a wildcard domain identifier.
