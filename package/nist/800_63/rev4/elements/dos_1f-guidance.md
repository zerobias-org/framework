The purpose of this initial step is to establish a common understanding of the online service's context and functionality, which is essential for informing the subsequent risk assessment steps of the DIRM process.

The set of entities includes users of the online service, organizations, and populations served.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if a description of the online service has been developed that includes the set of entities that will be impacted by the online service and the broader business process of which it is a part.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the organization's DIRM documentation for each online service to ensure it includes  the set of entities that will be impacted by the online service and the broader business process of which it is a part.
