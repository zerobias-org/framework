**Applies to:** CSP/IdP · **Topic:** Acceptance supplement · **Section:** SP 800-63-4 3.4.4 E

The DIAS prepared by a CSP SHALL include, at a minimum:

- (a) Claimed xAL, related controls, and rationale for any deviations from normative guidance;
- (b) All compensating controls with their comparability or residual risks; and
- (c) All supplemental controls.
