**Assessment objective**

Determine if the CSP allows the use of applicant references and, if it does, confirm that all applicant references are identity-proofed to the same or higher IAL as the applicant or applicants they are supporting.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that it identity proofs applicant reference to the same or higher IAL as the applicants.
- EXAMINE: Examine one or more sample records of subscribers who were identity proofed using an applicant reference and determine the IAL associated with both the subscriber and their reference.
