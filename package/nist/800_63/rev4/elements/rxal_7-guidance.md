Assessment is required when: The xAL is within a range of possible values specified by the trust agreement

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP includes sufficient information in assertions for RPs to determine xALs when values vary within a range.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: For IALs: Satisfied by the variable-xAL case in RXAL-3. For AALs: Satisfied by the variable-xAL case in RXAL-4. For FALs: Satisfied by the variable-xAL case in RXAL-5.
