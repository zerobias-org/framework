**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP verifies the applicant's ownership of the strongest piece of evidence by one of the methods provided in Sec 4.3.6.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine how it verifies an applicant's ownership of the strongest piece of presented evidence.
- TEST: Test the CSP's IAL3 identity proofing workflow to determine that all documented verification processes for evidence are followed.
