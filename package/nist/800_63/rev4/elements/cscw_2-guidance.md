The trust agreement may be composed of information published independently by each party. The CSP publishes what it issues and the constraints it imposes on wallets. The wallet publishes its own capabilities and security practices. Together these constitute the trust agreement.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the trust agreement between the CSP and subscriber-controlled wallets contains the minimum required terms.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: For CSPs: Examine CSP-published information to verify that terms (1), (3), and (7) are present.
- EXAMINE: For wallets (IdPs): Examine wallet-published information to verify terms (2), (4), (5), and (6) are present.
