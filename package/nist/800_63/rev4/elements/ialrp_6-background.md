**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.6.1.1 F

If additional attributes are requested by the RP, the request SHALL be:

- (a) Subject to a runtime decision of the authorized party to approve the additional attributes requested.
- (b) Redacted to only the attributes in the allowlist entry, or
- (c) Denied outright by the IdP.
