Applies to federal agencies and those acting on their behalf (e.g., contractors) only.

**Assessment objective**

Verifiers operated by or on behalf of government agencies are required to be validated to meet FIPS 140 requirements. The FIPS 140 requirements generally apply to cryptographic modules (both hardware and software).

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine system documentation or software information to identify the cryptographic modules used by the verifiers and verify associated CMVP validation certificates.
