**Applies to:** CSP/IdP, RP · **Topic:** Continuous evaluation and improvement · **Section:** SP 800-63-4 3.5.1 A

At a minimum, evaluation inputs SHALL include:

- (a) Integrated CSP, IdP, and authentication functions as well as validation, verification, and fraud management systems, as appropriate;
- (b) Customer feedback mechanisms, such as complaint processes, helpdesk statistics, and other user feedback (e.g., surveys, interviews, or focus groups);
- (c) Threat analysis, threat reporting, and threat intelligence feeds that are available;
- (d) Fraud trends, fraud investigation results, and fraud metrics as available; and
- (e) The results of ongoing customer experience assessments and privacy assessments.
