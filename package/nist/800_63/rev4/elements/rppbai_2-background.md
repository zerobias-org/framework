**Applies to:** RP · **Assurance level:** FAL3 · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 3.16.1 B

The system administrator of the RP SHALL use an independent means to determine whether the identified subject of the RP subscriber account is the party to which the authenticator is issued. The system administrator of the RP SHALL follow the initial authenticator binding requirements in Sec. 4 of [SP800-63A] or the post-enrollment binding requirements in Sec. 4.1.2 of [SP800-63B], as appropriate.
