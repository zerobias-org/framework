**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP assesses its proofing agents and trusted referees on their ability to conduct visual facial image comparisons.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that it assesses its proofing agents and/or trusted referees on their ability to conduct visual facial image comparisons.
- INTERVIEW: Interview one or more proofing agents or trusted referees to determine that they have been assessed on their ability to conduct visual facial image comparisons.
