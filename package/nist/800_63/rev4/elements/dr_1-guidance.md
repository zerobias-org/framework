**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP associates the IdP's public signing key(s) and configuration information with the IdP's identifier per the trust agreement artifact(s).

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the RP's federation configuration and discovery/registration to determine that the RP associates the IdP's assertion validation key(s), and other relevant IdP-specific configuration information with the IdP's identifier, as stipulated by the trust agreement artifacts.
