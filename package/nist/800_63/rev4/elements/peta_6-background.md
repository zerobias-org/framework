**Applies to:** IdP/RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.3.1 F

If shared signaling is used by the IdP or RP (see Sec. 4.8), the terms of the trust agreement SHALL establish: the events that trigger a signal to be sent; which signals are sent for each trigger; the information included in each signal; and the expected behavior of the party receiving the signal.
