Assessment is required when: The assessment target (IdP or RP) is a federal agency.

This control applies when PRIVR-1 analysis determines that the Privacy Act is triggered. If the SAOP has determined that the Privacy Act is triggered, the agency is required to either publish or identify existing coverage by a SORN.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the agency has published or identified coverage by a SORN, as applicable.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the agency's SORN documentation to verify that federation activities are covered by either a dedicated SORN or an existing programmatic SORN.
