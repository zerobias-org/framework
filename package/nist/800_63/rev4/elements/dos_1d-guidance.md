The purpose of this initial step is to establish a common understanding of the online service's context and functionality, which is essential for informing the subsequent risk assessment steps of the DIRM process.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if a description of the online service has been developed that includes the functionality of the online service and the data that it is expected to process.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the organization's DIRM documentation for each online service to ensure it includes its functionality and the data that it is expected to process.
