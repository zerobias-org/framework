This is the CSP counterpart to RR-1 (RP Subscriber Redress) and RR-3 (IdP Subscriber Redress). This control addresses redress for matters solely within the CSP's control.

"Clear and accessible" means the redress mechanism must be easy to find and use. Complex processes, hidden contact information, or technical jargon that prevents subscribers from understanding how to seek redress would fail this requirement.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the CSP provides a clear and accessible means for subscribers to seek redress for matters involving the CSP subscriber account.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine CSP documentation, subscriber-facing interfaces, and help resources for redress mechanisms. Verify that redress mechanisms are clearly presented and accessible to subscribers, and that all potential areas of redress are addressed, including the CSP subscriber account, identity attributes (if collected), and authenticators. Review CSP documentation to determine whether there are any other items under the CSP's control, and if so, verify that redress mechanisms are sufficient.
- TEST: Test the redress process by attempting to locate and initiate it as a test subscriber to verify accessibility.
