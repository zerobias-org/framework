Validated evidence is the preferred source of identity attributes. If the presented identity evidence does not provide all of the attributes that the CSP considers to be core attributes, it MAY collect attributes that are self-asserted by the applicant.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP collects all core attributes, including at least one government identifier.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy for collecting core attributes.
- TEST: Test the CSP's  identity proofing workflow to determine that all documented core attribute collection processes are followed.
