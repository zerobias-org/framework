**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP validates each piece of FAIR or STRONG evidence using the one of the techniques provided in item #1 of Sec. 4.3.4.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy and process for validating FAIR and STRONG evidence at IAL3.
- TEST: Test the CSP's IAL3 identity proofing workflow to determine that all documented validation processes for FAIR and STRONG evidence are followed.
