Assessment is required when: The RP collects additional attributes not provided by the IdP.

This control requires the RP to adhere to the purpose disclosed under CAARP-2.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP limits use of additional attributes to the stated purposes of its functionality.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine RP policies and procedures for handling additional attributes to verify that use is limited to the purposes disclosed per CAARP-2.
