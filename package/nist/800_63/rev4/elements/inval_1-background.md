**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 4.5 A

CSPs SHALL promptly invalidate authenticators when a subscriber account ceases to exist (e.g., subscriber's death, the discovery of a fraudulent subscriber), when requested by the subscriber, when the authenticator is compromised, or when the CSP determines that the subscriber no longer meets its eligibility requirements.
