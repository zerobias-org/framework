**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 3.11 #2a

When collecting and comparing biometric characteristics remotely, the CSP SHALL implement presentation attack detection (PAD) capabilities that meet the impostor attack presentation accept rate (IAPAR) performance metric of <0.07 to confirm the genuine presence of a live human being and to mitigate spoofing and impersonation attempts.
