Assessment is required when: The RP may receive a signal from an IdP indicating that a subscriber account has been terminated or the subscriber's access to the RP has been revoked.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP processes the RP subscriber account in accordance with the trust agreement artifact(s) and Section 3.11.3 upon receiving a termination or revocation signal from the IdP.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the trust agreement artifact(s) to identify the stipulated processing requirements for termination and revocation signals. Review RP account termination procedures to verify alignment with those requirements, and with SSIN-1, SSIN-2, and SSIN-3.
- TEST: Test by sending a termination signal to the RP and verifying that the RP subscriber account is processed according to the requirements for termination and revocation signals stipulated in the trust agreement artifact(s), and in accordance with SSIN-1, SSIN-2, and SSIN-3.
