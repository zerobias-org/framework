**Assessment objective**

For evidence with MRZs or barcodes, confirm that the CSP employs optical capture and inspection capabilities that compare the MRZ data with the printed data on the evidence.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's design or other documentation to determine that it employs optical capture and inspection capabilities that are able to compare evidence MRZ data to printed data.
- TEST: Test the CSPs implemented evidence validation technology to confirm it compares the MRZ to the printed data.
