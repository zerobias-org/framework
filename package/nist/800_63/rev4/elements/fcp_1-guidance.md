Assessment is required when: Front-channel presentation is used.

Front-channel presentation passes the full assertion through the subscriber's browser (user agent), increasing the risk of capture and replay. This requirement mandates that the RP track assertion identifiers to ensure each assertion is accepted at most once within its validity window.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP uses the assertion identifier to prevent replay within the assertion's validity time window.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Satisfied by FAL1-5.
