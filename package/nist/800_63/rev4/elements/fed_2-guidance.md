**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the Federal Agency has consulted with its SAOP to determine if the collection of personal information in association with the use of the identity service triggers E-Government Act of 2002 requirements.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the Federal Agency's documentation to determine if it has consulted with its SAOP whether its use of an identity service triggers requirements under the E-Government Act of 2002.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine whether the Federal Agency has consulted with is SAOP and whether its use of an identity service triggers requirements under the E-Government Act of 2002.
