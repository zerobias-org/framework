See Sec. 3.6 of [SP800-63] for more information about redress.

**Assessment objective**

Determine that the CSP has established redress procedures specific to issues associated with its fraud checks and mitigation measures.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to confirm it provides a way for applicants to seek redress to resolve issues associated with the CSP's fraud checks or other fraud mitigation measures.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine how the CSP provides a way for applicants to seek redress to resolve issues associated with the CSP's fraud checks or other fraud mitigation measures.
