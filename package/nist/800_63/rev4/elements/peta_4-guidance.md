See Sec. 3.5.3 (RR-1 to RR-6) for additional requirements and considerations for redress mechanisms.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the assessed party has evaluated its redress mechanisms and disclosed the assessment results in trust agreement artifact(s).

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the relevant trust agreement artifact(s) to verify that they include the results of the party's assessment of the efficacy of its redress mechanism, and that the information has been made available to the other party.
