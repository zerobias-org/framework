Assessment is required when: The IdP provides attributes through a provisioning API.

Provisioning APIs can expose a broader set of subscriber attributes than would typically be transmitted in individual federation transactions. The IdP must ensure that only the minimum necessary attributes are made available through this channel, consistent with data minimization principles.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP minimizes the attributes made available to the RP through the provisioning API.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Satisfied by TSI-1.
