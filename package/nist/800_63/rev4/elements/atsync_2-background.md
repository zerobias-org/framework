**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.6.4 B

If the reason for termination is suspicious or fraudulent activity, the IdP SHALL notify the RP of the termination and include the reason in its signal to the RP to allow the RP to review the associated RP subscriber account's activity for suspicious activity, if specified in the trust agreement with that RP.
