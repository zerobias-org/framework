**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the organization documents its provisioning model in the trust agreement artifact(s).

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the  trust agreement artifact(s) for documentation of the provisioning model(s) in use. Verify that each documented model is identifiable as one of the provisioning methods enumerated in Sec. 4.6.3: just-in-time, pre-provisioning, ephemeral, or an alternative method.
