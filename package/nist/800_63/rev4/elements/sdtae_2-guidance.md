Assessment is required when: The subscriber is the authorized party.

See Sec. 3.5.3 for additional requirements and considerations for redress mechanisms. (RR-1, RR-3, RR-4, RR-6)

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP has assessed the efficacy of its redress mechanisms and discloses the results to subscribers.

**Assessment methods**

- TEST: The redress mechanism efficacy assessment is satisfied by PETA-4. Test for disclosure to the subscriber by requesting the results of the redress mechanism efficacy assessment, as a test subscriber. Verify that the results are provided.
