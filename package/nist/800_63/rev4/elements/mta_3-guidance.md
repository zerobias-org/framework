Assessment is required when: A federation authority facilitates the inclusion of CSPs, IDPs, and RPs under a trust agreement.

This is the first of six minimum vetting criteria specified in Section 3.5.2. The federation authority's vetting process must verify that CSPs perform identity proofing consistent with SP 800-63A requirements for the applicable IAL(s) supported by the federation.

SP 800-63A-4 establishes requirements for identity proofing at IAL1, IAL2, and IAL3, including evidence collection, validation, and verification processes. The depth of vetting should be commensurate with the IALs the federation supports.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the federation authority's vetting process establishes that CSPs identity proof subscriber accounts in accordance with SP 800-63A.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine federation authority vetting procedures, checklists, and assessment criteria to verify that CSP identity proofing practices are evaluated against SP 800-63A requirements for each IAL offered by the CSP.
