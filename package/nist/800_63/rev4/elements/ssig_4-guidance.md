Assessment is required when: Shared signaling is used.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether shared signaling has been subject to privacy review under the trust agreement.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine each party's privacy risk assessment (or equivalent privacy review documentation) to verify that shared signaling is addressed, including the events that trigger signals, any personal information transmitted, and the expected processing by the receiving party. Refer to SSIG-2 and PETA-6 for the documented signaling terms that should be covered by the privacy review.
