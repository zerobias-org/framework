**Assessment objective**

Determine that the RP has reviewed and accepted all risks associated with fraud mitigation measures employed by its CSP as compensating controls and confirmed they align with the RP's own risk acceptance profile.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the documented results of any reviews of the risks associated with any fraud mitigation measures used as compensating controls by its CSPs.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine that the RP has reviewed and accepted the risks associated with any fraud mitigation measures used as compensating controls by its CSPs.
