The RP MAY provide a process for associating subscriber-provided authenticators to the RP subscriber account on a trust-on-first-use basis. This process is known as a binding ceremony and has additional requirements beyond a typical FAL3 federation process.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the RP performs a binding ceremony to establish the connection between the authenticator, the subscriber, and the RP subscriber account, as shown in Fig. 5, when no bound authenticators are associated with the RP subscriber account.
