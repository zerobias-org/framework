**Assessment objective**

Determine that the CSP has conducted a risk assessment and that it employs appropriate security controls consistent with the NIST SP 800-53 MODERATE baseline or greater.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's risk assessment results document, or other documentation, to confirm it has conducted a security risk assessment.
- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's identity service design document, certification information, or other documentation, to determine if it employs MODERATE baseline controls or greater.
