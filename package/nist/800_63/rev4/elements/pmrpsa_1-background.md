**Applies to:** RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 4.6.3 A

The RP subscriber account SHALL be provisioned at the RP prior to the establishment of an authenticated session at the RP in one of the following ways:

- (a) Just-In-Time Provisioning
- (b) Pre-Provisioning
- (c) Ephemeral Provisioning
- (d) Other Provisioning Methods
