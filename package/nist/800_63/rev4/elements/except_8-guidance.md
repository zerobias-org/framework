CSPs SHOULD offer trusted referee services for failures in completing automated validation processes, such as mismatched core attributes or the absence of the applicant in a record source.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if the CSP offers trusted referees to assist with automated validation failures and, if it does, confirm that their involvement in these scenarios adheres to the specified requirements.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy on trusted referee involvement in automated validation failures.
