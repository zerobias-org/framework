**Applies to:** RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 5.1 C

The continuity of authenticated sessions SHALL be based on the possession of a session secret that is issued by the session host at the time of authentication and optionally refreshed during the session. The nature of a session depends on the application, such as: a web browser session with a "session" cookie; and an instance of a mobile application that retains a session secret.
