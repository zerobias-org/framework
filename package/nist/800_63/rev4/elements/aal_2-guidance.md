Applies to federal agencies only.

**Assessment objective**

Comply with Section 3 of Executive Order 13681 requiring multifactor authentication

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: For federal agency relying parties, where personal information is made available online, Examine policies defining accepted authentication methods to determine that all meet a minimum of AAL2.
- TEST: Test online authentication processes for identified applications that handle personal information to confirm AAL2 authentication policies are enforced.
