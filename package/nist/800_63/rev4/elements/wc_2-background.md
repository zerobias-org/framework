**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 3.2.11.2 B

Wireless connections SHALL establish a key for encrypted communication between the authenticator and endpoint in one of the following ways:

- (1) Through a temporary wired connection between the devices.
- (2) Through an association process that is similar to a pairing process but does not require a persistent relationship between devices to establish a key for encrypted communication between the authenticator and endpoint.
