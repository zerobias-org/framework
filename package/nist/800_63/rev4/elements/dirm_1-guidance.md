For relying parties, the intent of [the DIRM] process is to determine the assurance levels and any tailoring required to protect all online services and the applications, transactions, and systems that comprise or are impacted by those services. This directly contributes to the selection, development, and procurement of CSP services.

**Assessment objective**

Determine if all online services of the Federal RP are identified and ensure the DIRM process is implemented for those online services.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine DIRM policy to determine that it is intended to cover all organizational applications. If available, review the  list of online services to ensure all services are identified and that DIRM documentation for each online service exists.
