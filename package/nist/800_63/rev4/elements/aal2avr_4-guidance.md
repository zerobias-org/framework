**Assessment objective**

Determine that the communication channel meets the requirements of an authenticated protected channel as defined in SP 800-63B-4 Appendix D.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the verifier's documentation to ensure that TLS or a similarly secure protocol is used in conjunction with an approved encryption protocol.
- TEST: Test the verifier's interactions and observe that TLS or similarly secure protocol is used in conjunction with an approved encryption protocol.
