Behavioral analytics in this context is used to determine whether an interaction is indicative of an automated attack and not an effort to identify or authenticate a specific user based on a captured reference template for that user.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP has identified which automated attacks its identity service is vulnerable to and has implemented appropriate attack detection mechanisms.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's identity service design document, or other documentation, to determine that it employs appropriate automated attack detection mechanisms.
- TEST: Test whether the identity service is able to detect automated attacks.
