**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 5.3.3 A

Subscriber-controlled wallets SHALL disclose to RPs:

- (1) Activation or authentication methods they use to authenticate subscribers prior to presentation of an assertion.
- (2) Security features of how the wallet protects the attribute bundles.
- (3) Key management information.
- (4) Indications of the integrity of the subscriber-controlled wallet's software.
