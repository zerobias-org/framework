CSPs SHOULD have their evidence validation technology periodically tested by independent entities (e.g., accredited laboratories or research institutions) for their performance characteristic.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP makes publicly available the results of any performance assessments it conducts on its image capture and validation technologies.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that it makes publicly available the results of any performance assessments it conducts on its image capture and validation technologies.
- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's performance assessment results on a publicly available website or platform.
