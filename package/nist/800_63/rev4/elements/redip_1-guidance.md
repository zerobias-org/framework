See section 3.6 of NIST SP 800-63-4 for more information about redress.

**Assessment objective**

Determine that the CSP provides a mechanism for applicants to seek redress for errors or harms associated with their use of the CSP's identity service.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement, or other documentation, to determine its redress mechanism(s).
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine the CSP's redress mechanism(s).
