**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** AAL3 · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 4.2.2.3 A

If an account that can authenticate at AAL3 has been identity-proofed at IAL3, the CSP SHALL successfully perform a successful biometric comparison against the biometric characteristic collected during an initial on-site attended identity proofing session, as described in [SP800-63A].
