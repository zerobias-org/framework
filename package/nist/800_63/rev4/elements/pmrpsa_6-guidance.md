Assessment is required when: An Alternative Provisioning Method is used.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the privacy risk assessments of the IdP and RP include the details of any alternative provisioning model in use.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the privacy risk assessment to verify that it addresses the alternative provisioning model, including a description of the alternative provisioning model and the privacy risks specific to the model (e.g., data exposure, retention, minimization).
