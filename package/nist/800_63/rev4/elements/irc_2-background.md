**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 4.2.1.2 B

Issued recovery codes SHALL be valid for at most:

- (a) 21 days when sent to a postal address within the contiguous United States.
- (b) 30 days when sent to a postal address outside of the contiguous United States.
- (c) 10 minutes when sent via text message or voice.
- (d) 24 hours when sent to an email address.
