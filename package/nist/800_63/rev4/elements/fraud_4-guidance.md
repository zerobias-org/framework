**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP monitors ID proofing communication channels for indicators of fraud or attacks.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's design documentation or its documented procedures to determine that it monitors and analyzes all remote ID proofing communication channels for evidence of fraudulent activity or attacks.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine how the CSP monitors its remote ID proofing channels for indicators of fraud or attacks.
