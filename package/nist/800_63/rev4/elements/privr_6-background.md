**Applies to:** RP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 3.10 A

If the RP subscriber account life cycle process gives the RP access to attributes through a provisioning API (see Sec. 4.6.3), additional privacy measures SHALL be implemented to account for the difference in the RP subscriber account life cycle (e.g., separation of non-active subscriber accounts, proactive removal of disabled and terminated accounts).
