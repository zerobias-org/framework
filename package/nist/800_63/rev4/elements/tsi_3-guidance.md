Assessment is required when: An IdP gathers the subscriber's consent to use information outside the identity transaction.

Subscriber consent must be meaningful. When IdPs use consent measures for processing beyond identity service functions, they cannot make subscriber acceptance of additional processing a condition of providing the identity service. For example, an IdP cannot require a subscriber to consent to marketing use of their data as a condition of federated login.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the IdP refrains from making consent for additional processing a condition of the identity service, when the IdP gathers subscriber consent to use information outside the identity transaction.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the IdP's consent mechanisms and user interface flows for gathering subscriber consent to additional processing. Verify that subscribers can decline consent for additional processing and still complete identity service functions.
- TEST: Test by attempting to use the identity service while declining consent for any additional processing. Verify that the identity service remains available.
