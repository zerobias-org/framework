**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 5.4 A

The subscriber-controlled wallet SHALL require the presentation of an activation factor from the subscriber for the following actions that result in the creation of a signed artifact from the wallet's signing keys: providing proof of possession of the wallet's signing key to the CSP during the issuance process; and signing the assertion for presentation to the RP.
