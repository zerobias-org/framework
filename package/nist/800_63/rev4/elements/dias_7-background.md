**Applies to:** RP, CSP/IdP · **Topic:** Acceptance supplement · **Section:** SP 800-63-4 3.4.4 G

The DIAS prepared by the organization for external online services SHALL include, at a minimum:

- (a) Assessed xAL, related controls, and rationale for any deviations from normative guidance;
- (b) All compensating controls with their comparability or residual risks; and
- (c) All supplemental controls.
