Validated evidence is the preferred source of identity attributes. If the presented identity evidence does not provide all of the attributes that a CSP considers to be core attributes, the CSP MAY collect attributes that are self asserted by the applicant.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP collects all core attributes, including at least one government identifier.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy for collecting core attributes.
- TEST: Test the CSP's IAL3 identity proofing workflow to determine that all documented core attribute collection processes are followed.
