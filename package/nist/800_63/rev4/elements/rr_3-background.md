**Applies to:** IdP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63C-4 3.5.3 C

For matters that involve the use of the subscriber account in federation transactions, including attribute values and derived attribute values made available over federation transactions, IdP functionality, holder-of-key authenticators, IdP allowlists, and other items in the IdP's control, the IdP SHALL provide a clear and accessible means of redress to the subscriber.
