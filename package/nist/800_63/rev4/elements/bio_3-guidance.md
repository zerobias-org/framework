**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP stores a record of the subscriber's consent for biometric use and that it is associated with the subscriber's account.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy for associating a record of the subscriber's consent for biometric use with their account.
- EXAMINE: Examine and test the subscriber account to determine that it includes a record of the subscriber's consent for biometric use.
