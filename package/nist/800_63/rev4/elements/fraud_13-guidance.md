**Assessment objective**

Confirm the RP has established a point of contact to receive fraud and other information from its CSPs.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine an example agreement or contract to determine that the RP has an identified point of contact (POC) to interact with and receive fraud and other information for CSPs.
- INTERVIEW: Interview appropriate personnel to determine that the RP has an identified POC to interact with and receive fraud and other information for CSPs.
