Assessment is required when: The assessment target (IdP or RP) is a federal agency.

This assessment focuses specifically on the risks associated with sharing subscriber identity information between federation parties. Unlike the Privacy Act and E-Government Act analyses (PRIVR-1, PRIVR-3), this assessment is required regardless of whether those statutes are triggered.

**Assessment objective**

Determine whether the agency has conducted a privacy risk assessment regarding the sharing of subscriber identity information between the IdP and RP.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the agency's privacy risk assessment documentation addressing the sharing of subscriber identity information in federation transactions.
