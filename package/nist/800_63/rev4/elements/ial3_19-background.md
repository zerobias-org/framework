**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** IAL3 · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 4.3.8 #5

For kiosk-based on-site attended identity proofing, all devices used to support interaction between the proofing agent and the applicant SHALL be safeguarded from tampering through observation by CSP representatives or monitoring devices (e.g., cameras) and through physical and digital tamper prevention features.
