Documentation of the complete service description allows CSPs to communicate to RPs and others the scope and components of their services and how they achieve specific IALs.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP has documented the particular steps it employs to identity proof applicants at each offered assurance level.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine it has documented the steps it follows to identity proof applicants at a specified assurance level.
