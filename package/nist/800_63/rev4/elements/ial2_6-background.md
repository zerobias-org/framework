**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** IAL2 · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 4.2.5 #1

The CSP SHALL validate all core attributes by either:

- (a) Comparing the government identifier and other core attributes against an authoritative or credible source to determine accuracy.
- (b) Validating the accuracy of digitally signed attributes that are contained on SUPERIOR evidence through the public key of the issuing source.
