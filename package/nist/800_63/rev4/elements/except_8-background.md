**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 3.15.2 #3

If a CSP offers trusted referees [are offered for failures in completing automated validation processes], the following requirements apply:

- (a) CSPs SHALL provide a policy for additional evidence types that may be used to corroborate core attributes or changes in core attributes.
- (b) Trusted referees SHALL review additional evidence types for authenticity to the greatest degree allowed by the evidence.
- (c) If no authoritative or credible records are available to support validation, the trusted referee MAY compare the attributes on additional pieces of evidence with the strongest piece of evidence available to corroborate the consistency of core attributes.
- (d) If there is a partial mismatch of core attributes to authoritative records, the trusted referee SHALL review evidence that supports the legitimacy of the asserted attribute value (e.g., recent move or change of name).
