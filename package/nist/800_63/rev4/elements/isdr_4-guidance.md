Lack of timeliness results in the degradation of mission delivery as it affects the ability of the organization to perform mission functions and increases burden on applicants seeking services. Providing policies for the timely completion of identity proofing services sets expectations for RPs and enables communication to applicants about the anticipated timing of the process.

**Assessment objective**

Confirm that the CSP has a documented policy for ensuring the identity proofing process concludes in a timely manner.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine its policy for the timely conclusion of the identity proofing process.
