**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** AAL2 · **Section:** SP 800-63B-4 3.1.5.1 G

The unencrypted key and activation secret or biometric sample and any biometric data derived from the biometric sample (e.g., a fingerprint image and feature locations produced by a fingerprint feature extractor) SHALL be erased immediately after an OTP has been generated.
