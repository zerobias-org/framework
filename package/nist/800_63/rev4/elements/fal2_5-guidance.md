At FAL2 and higher, identifiers must be privacy-preserving and non-identifying. This protects subscriber privacy if assertions are intercepted.

**Assessment objective**

Verify that, at FAL2 and above, the IdP issues federated identifiers that do not include any plaintext personal information such as usernames, email addresses, employee numbers, or other directly identifying data.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the IdP's federated identifier construction rules, documentation, and representative issued identifiers to determine whether any federated identifier contains plaintext personal information, such as usernames, email addresses, employee numbers, names, phone numbers, or similar directly identifying values.
- TEST: Test by generating representative identifiers for sample subscriber accounts and verifying that no identifier reveals plaintext personal information.
