**Assessment objective**

Confirm that, for on-site biometric collection as part of the identity proofing process, human agents of the CSP examine the source of the biometric (e.g., fingers, face) for the presence of any unexpected, non-natural materials.

**Assessment methods**

- EXAMINE: Examine the CSP's practices statement or other documentation to determine that, for during on-site identity proofing processes, it has human operators view the source of any collected biometrics for the source of unexpected foreign materials.
