**Applies to:** CSP · **Assurance level:** ALL · **Section:** SP 800-63A-4 3.5 #4

The CSP SHALL assess the information security and privacy risks associated with operating its identity service, according to the NIST Risk Management Framework or equivalent risk management guidelines. At a minimum, the CSP SHALL apply appropriate controls consistent with the NIST SP 800-53 moderate baseline, regardless of IAL.
