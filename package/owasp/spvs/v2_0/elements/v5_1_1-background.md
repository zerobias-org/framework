**Requirement:** V5.1.1 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V5 Operate · **Sub-category:** V5.1 Access Audit

Verify that user access entitlements to pipeline systems, including version control, continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) platforms, artifact registries, and secret stores, are reviewed on a documented cadence.
