**Requirement:** V1.3.9 · **Level:** 1 (applies at Level 1 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.3 Identity and Access Management

Verify that every account with publish rights to a package requires phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication (MFA), enforced for each package and not satisfied by an organization-level setting alone.
