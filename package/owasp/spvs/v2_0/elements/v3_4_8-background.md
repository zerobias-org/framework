**Requirement:** V3.4.8 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.4 Credential Hygiene

Verify that each project has its own pipeline secrets, scoped to only what that project needs, and that no secret is shared across an entire organization or across all repositories.

**Tags:** secrets; access-control
