**Requirement:** V3.13.10 · **Level:** 3 (applies at Level 3 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that each pipeline stage checks the signature of any artifact it receives from the stage before it against a known trust source, and that the pipeline run fails when the check fails.
