**Requirement:** V1.6.5 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.6 Developer Tool Operation

Verify that IDE extensions are limited to a centrally approved list, pinned to a specific version, and verified by checksum at install and update, with auto-update disabled or routed through the same approval and verification process.
