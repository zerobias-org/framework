**Requirement:** V3.1.11 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.1 Security of Pipeline Environment

Verify that CI/CD runners are ephemeral and destroyed after each job, or that persistent runners are re-imaged on a defined schedule and scanned for tampering before reuse.
