**Requirement:** V1.6.6 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.6 Developer Tool Operation

Verify that developer tool installations, configuration files, and extension or add-on inventory are monitored for unauthorized changes, and that alerts are routed to a documented response process.
