**Requirement:** V3.13.11 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that build outputs include a signed provenance attestation that binds the artifact to its source, builder identity, and build parameters, and that downstream consumers validate this attestation before use.
