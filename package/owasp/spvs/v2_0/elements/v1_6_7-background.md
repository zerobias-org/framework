**Requirement:** V1.6.7 · **Level:** 3 (applies at Level 3 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.6 Developer Tool Operation

Verify that runtime communication destinations of installed developer tool extensions and add-ons are governed by a destination allowlist or a documented destination policy with periodic review, and that communication with a destination outside the allowlist or policy triggers an alert.

**Tags:** developer-tools; network; monitoring
