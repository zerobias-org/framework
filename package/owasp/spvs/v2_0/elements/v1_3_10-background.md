**Requirement:** V1.3.10 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.3 Identity and Access Management

Verify that no single credential can write to more than one repository, package, or service. Break broad-access credentials into smaller, single-purpose ones.
