**Requirement:** V3.13.12 · **Level:** 3 (applies at Level 3 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that every published artifact is automatically compared against the output of its release pipeline, including declared version, embedded build metadata, and content digest, and that any divergence triggers an alert and blocks promotion.
