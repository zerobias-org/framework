**Requirement:** V3.4.7 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.4 Credential Hygiene

Verify that secrets given to a shared or reusable pipeline are limited to the one job that needs them, instead of being automatically passed to every job in the chain.
