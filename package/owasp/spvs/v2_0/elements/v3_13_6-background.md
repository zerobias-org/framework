**Requirement:** V3.13.6 · **Level:** 3 (applies at Level 3 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that build provenance attestations are validated against an expected baseline for recorded builder identity and isolation state as well as signature, and that an attestation missing these fields or not matching the baseline fails validation.
