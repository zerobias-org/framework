**Requirement:** V3.13.4 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) cache contents are signed or checksummed at write time, and that the resulting signature, checksum, or verification key is stored in a trust boundary separate from the cache.
