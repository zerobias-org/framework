**Requirement:** V3.1.10 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.1 Security of Pipeline Environment

Verify that every container base image used in a build or CI step is pinned to a specific digest, and that the pipeline fails when the digest does not match the expected value.
