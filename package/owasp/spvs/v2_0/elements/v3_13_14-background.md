**Requirement:** V3.13.14 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that the software bill of materials (SBOM) for each release is validated against an approved machine-readable format before deployment, and that deployment is blocked when the SBOM is missing or fails validation.
