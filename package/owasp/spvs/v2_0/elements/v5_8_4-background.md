**Requirement:** V5.8.4 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V5 Operate · **Sub-category:** V5.8 Incident Response & Recovery

Verify that when a credential is rotated because of an incident, the compromised credential is revoked before the replacement is activated, and within a defined SLO.
