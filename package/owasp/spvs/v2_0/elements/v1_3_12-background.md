**Requirement:** V1.3.12 · **Level:** 1 (applies at Level 1 and above) · **Phase:** V1 Plan · **Sub-category:** V1.3 Identity and Access Management

Verify that CI/CD workflow permissions default to read-only at the platform or organization level, and that write permissions are granted only per workflow with documented justification.
