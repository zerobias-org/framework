**Requirement:** V3.13.5 · **Level:** 2 (applies at Level 2 and above) · **Phase:** V3 Integrate (CI*) · **Sub-category:** V3.13 Integrity of Artifacts

Verify that restored Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) cache contents are checked against their write-time integrity value before use, and that the build fails when the check fails or when the integrity value is stored with the cache.

**Tags:** integrity; build
