Mechanisms exist to identify and catalog the organization's High Value Assets (HVAs) (e.g.,  crown jewels), including defining:

- (1) Criteria for high-value Intellectual Property (IP) to be categorized as a HVA;
- (2) Criteria for Technology Assets, Applications and Services (TAAS) to be categorized as a HVA based on business process criticality or dependency relationships;
- (3) Location of HVA Technology Assets, Applications, Services and/or Data (TAASD);
- (4) Assigned owners;
- (5) Minimum protection mechanisms that must be implemented; and
- (6) Assurance requirements.
