Mechanisms exist to maintain a current inventory of Technology Assets, Applications and/or Services (TAAS) with Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) exposure, including:

- (1) Public key algorithms vulnerable to Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQCs);
- (2) Long-lived keys and certificates (e.g., CA roots, firmware signing keys, etc.); and
- (3) TAAS that cannot easily adopt PQC upgrades (e.g., embedded, RTOS, etc.).
