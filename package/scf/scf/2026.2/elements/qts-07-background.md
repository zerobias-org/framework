Mechanisms exist to establish the capability to respond to the compromise or disallowance of a Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) or classical algorithm on a compressed timeline, including:

- (1) Pre-identified algorithm alternates;
- (2) Tested rollback and roll-forward procedures;
- (3) Customer and/or counterparty communication templates; and
- (4) Incident response rehearsals against defined scenarios.
