Mechanisms exist to leverage hybrid/composite algorithms as a transition path to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) solutions that:

- (1) Support hybrid signatures (e.g., ECDSA + ML-DSA) and hybrid Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (KEMs) (e.g., ECDH + ML-KEM);
- (2) Ensure certificate formats, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) and trust anchors can support dual-key or dual-certificate models; and
- (3) Plan for eventual removal of classical algorithms once PQC confidence is sufficient.
