Mechanisms exist to update contingency plans due to changes affecting:

- (1) People (e.g., personnel changes);
- (2) Processes (e.g., new, altered or decommissioned business practices, including third-party services)
- (3) Technologies (e.g., new, altered or decommissioned technologies);
- (4) Data (e.g., changes to data flows and/or data repositories);
- (5) Facilities (e.g., new, altered or decommissioned physical infrastructure); and/or
- (6) Feedback from contingency plan testing activities.
