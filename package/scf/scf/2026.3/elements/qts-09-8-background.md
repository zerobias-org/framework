Mechanisms exist to configure Technology Assets, Applications and/or Services (TAAS) to prevent attackers from forcing quantum-vulnerable algorithms through:

- (1) Integrity-protected algorithm negotiation (e.g., TLS 1.3 handshake transcript);
- (2) Disallowing negotiation of classical-only cipher suites once Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) is deployed; and
- (3) Monitoring for downgrade attempts.
