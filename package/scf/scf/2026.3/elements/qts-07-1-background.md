Mechanisms exist to maintain a current inventory of cryptographic assets that includes:

- (1) Algorithms (asymmetric and symmetric);
- (2) Key lengths;
- (3) Libraries;
- (4) Protocols;
- (5) Associated Technology Assets, Applications and/or Services (TAAS) utilizing the cryptography; and
- (6) Federal Information Processing Standards (FIPS) validation status from the Cryptographic Module Validation Program (CMVP), including certificate number, if applicable.
