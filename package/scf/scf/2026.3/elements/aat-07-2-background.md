Mechanisms exist to assign defined classes to Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Technologies (AAT) and AI agents based on their characteristics (e.g., intended use, autonomy, access, potential impact and risk) to determine applicable:

- (1) Approval requirements;
- (2) Security, compliance and/or resilience controls;
- (3) Testing rigor;
- (4) Monitoring requirements;
- (5) Supporting documentation; and
- (6) Oversight requirements.
