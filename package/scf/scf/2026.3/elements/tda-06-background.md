Mechanisms exist to design and implement product management processes to proactively govern the design, development and production of Technology Assets, Applications and/or Services (TAAS) across the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) to:

- (1) Improve functionality;
- (2) Enhance security and resiliency capabilities;
- (3) Correct security deficiencies; and
- (4) Conform with applicable statutory, regulatory and/or contractual obligations.
