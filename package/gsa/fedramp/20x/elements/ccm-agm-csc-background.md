**SHOULD:** Agencies SHOULD consider the Security Category noted in their Authorization to Operate of the federal information system that includes the cloud service offering in its boundary and assign appropriate information security resources for reviewing Ongoing Certification Reports, attending Quarterly Reviews, and other ongoing FedRAMP Certification Data.

**Applies to:** Class B, Class C (Agencies)

**Audience:** Agency Guidance. These rules for agencies apply to all agencies using a FedRAMP Certification.

## Defined terms

- **Certification Data:** The collective information required by FedRAMP for initial and ongoing FedRAMP Certification of a cloud service offering, including the FedRAMP Certification Package.
- **Cloud Service Offering:** A specific, packaged cloud computing product or service supplied by a cloud service provider for use by customers, that is the subject of a FedRAMP Certification.
- **Ongoing Certification:** The continued FedRAMP Certification of a cloud service offering based on the applicable FedRAMP Practices.
- **Quarterly Review:** A regular synchronous meeting hosted by a FedRAMP Certified cloud service provider for agency customers, following FedRAMP Collaborative Continuous Monitoring rules.
- **Security Category:** Has the meaning from NIST FIPS 199, which is "The characterization of information or an information system based on an assessment of the potential impact that a loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability of such information or information system would have on organizational operations, organizational assets, or individuals." Security categories are often referred to as "impact levels" and include Low, Moderate, and High.
