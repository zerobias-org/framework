**MUST:** FedRAMP MUST periodically review FedRAMP Incident Evaluation and Communication implementation with providers based on lack of reporting or other information.

**Applies to:** Class B, Class C (FedRAMP)

**Audience:** FedRAMP Responsibilities. These rules apply to FedRAMP.

### Corrective actions

- FedRAMP will request a Corrective Action Plan when a provider is unaware of the rules or has failed to implement proper procedures.
- FedRAMP will grant a 3 month grace period to implement proper procedures pending remediation and possible revocation of FedRAMP Certification.

## Defined terms

- **Incident:** Has the meaning given in 44 USC § 3552 (b)(2) which is "an occurrence that (A) actually or imminently jeopardizes, without lawful authority, the integrity, confidentiality, or availability of information or an information system; or (B) constitutes a violation or imminent threat of violation of law, security policies, security procedures, or acceptable use policies."
