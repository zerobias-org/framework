**MAY:** Providers MAY ask for and accept advice from their assessor during assessment regarding techniques and procedures that will improve their security posture or the effectiveness, clarity, and accuracy of their verification, validation and reporting procedures, UNLESS doing so is likely to compromise the objectivity and integrity of the assessment.

**Applies to:** Class B, Class C (Providers)

**Audience:** General Provider Responsibilities. These rules apply to cloud service providers obtaining and maintaining any FedRAMP Certification.

## Defined terms

- **Likely:** A reasonable degree of probability based on context.
- **Validation:** Confirmation through objective evidence that implemented security capabilities and related certification data are suitable for their intended FedRAMP Certification use and support the expected security outcomes for a cloud service offering.
- **Verification:** Confirmation through objective evidence that specified FedRAMP Practices have been fulfilled for a cloud service offering.
