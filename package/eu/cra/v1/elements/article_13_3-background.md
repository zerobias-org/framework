Where an importer considers or has reason to believe that a product with digital elements or the processes put in place by the manufacturer are not in conformity with the essential requirements set out in Annex I, the importer shall not place the product on the market until that product or the processes put in place by the manufacturer have been brought into conformity with the essential requirements set out in Annex I. Furthermore, where the product with digital elements presents a significant cybersecurity risk, the importer shall inform the manufacturer and the market surveillance authorities to that effect.
